PHENIBUT

β-Phenyl-GABA · Noofen · Anvifen
A Russian anxiolytic sold legally as a supplement. Produces genuine euphoria and social ease — and one of the fastest, most vicious dependency traps of any legal substance.
Critical Risk Onset: 2–4 hours (one of the slowest onset drugs) Duration: 10–24 hours
How It Works
Phenibut is a GABA-B agonist (same primary mechanism as baclofen and partially GHB) that also has GABA-A activity at high doses. Unlike most depressants, it doesn't cause significant sedation at low-moderate doses — it produces calm focus and reduced anxiety that many people find uniquely useful. At higher doses (1g+) it creates an alcohol-like euphoria without the sloppiness. The appeal is real. The problem: tolerance develops after just 2–3 consecutive uses, and withdrawal is disproportionately brutal.
Dosing
Anxiolytic 250–500mg Calm, social ease, mild mood lift
Recreational low 500–1000mg Noticeable euphoria, disinhibition
Recreational high 1000–2000mg Strong euphoria, heavy sedation
Too much 2000mg+ Memory impairment, next-day crash
Onset warning 2–4 hours Extremely slow — the #1 redose trap
Dangerous Interactions
☠️
Alcohol
Additive GABA-B activity. Dangerous sedation. Do not combine.
☠️
Benzos
Severe CNS depression risk. Both enhance GABA systems.
☠️
Opioids
Respiratory depression risk increases significantly.
⚠️
GHB
Both are GABA-B agonists. Synergistic and unpredictable.
ℹ️
Fasoracetam
May help restore GABA-B receptor sensitivity after phenibut use — used in recovery protocols.
Harm Reduction Tips
📅
Once per week absolute maximum
This rule is the difference between recreational use and dependency. After 2–3 consecutive days of use, tolerance and withdrawal set in. Once per week is not conservative — it's required.
⏱️
Set a 3-hour timer before redosing
The 2–4 hour onset is the single biggest cause of phenibut overdose. People think it's not working and redose. Then everything hits at once.
🧠
Expect the rebound
Day after will feel flat, anxious, and low-energy. This is normal. Do not redose to fix it — that's how the spiral starts.
📉
If dependent
Taper with baclofen (same mechanism, easier to titrate) under medical supervision. Do not cold turkey.
Withdrawal
Among the most severe and prolonged of any legal substance. Severe anxiety, insomnia, psychosis, agitation, and suicidal ideation have been reported. Withdrawal can last weeks to months. The once-per-week rule isn't about being cautious — it is the only way to use phenibut without eventually facing this outcome.
Addiction & Dependence
Phenibut is uniquely deceptive because the recreational experience is exceptionally good and it's legal and cheap. The dependency trap is fast (days) and the withdrawal is brutal and prolonged. Many people who become dependent didn't realize it was possible from a supplement. Treat it with more caution than street drugs.

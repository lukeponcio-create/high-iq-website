KAVA

Piper methysticum · Kava Kava · Yaqona
A Pacific Island ceremonial plant used safely for thousands of years. The lowest-risk recreational depressant on this page — no respiratory depression, no meaningful withdrawal, low addiction potential.
Low Risk Onset: 15–30 min Duration: 2–4 hours
How It Works
Kava's active compounds (kavalactones) work through multiple mechanisms: they modulate GABA-A receptors (weakly), block voltage-gated sodium and calcium channels, and interact with dopamine receptors. Crucially, unlike benzos and alcohol, kava does not cause significant respiratory depression even at high doses. This makes it genuinely safer. Effects are muscle relaxation, reduced anxiety, and mild euphoria without significant cognitive impairment at moderate doses.
Dosing
Mild 70–150mg KL Light relaxation, slightly numbed tongue
Moderate 150–300mg KL Calm euphoria, muscle relaxation, social ease
Strong 300–500mg KL 'Kava drunk' — heavy relaxation, sedation
Traditional 2–4 shells One shell ≈ 250ml prepared kava drink
Note Empty stomach Food significantly reduces absorption — wait 3+ hrs
Dangerous Interactions
☠️
Alcohol
Both metabolized by the liver. Increased hepatotoxicity risk. Also additive sedation. Avoid combining.
⚠️
Acetaminophen / Tylenol
Both processed by the liver. Avoid taking together.
⚠️
Benzos / opioids
Additive sedation. Not as dangerous as other combos but worth caution.
⚠️
Any medications
Kava inhibits CYP450 liver enzymes — can affect how many medications are metabolized. Check interactions.
ℹ️
Cannabis
Popular and generally safe combination. Both enhance relaxation. Can be quite sedating together.
Harm Reduction Tips
🍽️
Empty stomach only
Eat at least 3 hours before. This is the single biggest factor in whether kava works for you.
🔁
Reverse tolerance
First few sessions may feel nothing — this is normal. Keep trying on separate days. Once it 'clicks' it works reliably.
🌿
Noble strains only
Look for 'noble kava' designation. 'Tudei' kava causes harsher side effects and worse hangovers. Vendor research matters.
🏷️
Check KL content
Kavalactone percentage on the label tells you actual potency — more useful than grams of powder.
💊
Check drug interactions
If you take regular medications, look up CYP450 interactions with kava before using.
Withdrawal
Minimal to none for typical recreational use. Heavy chronic daily users may experience mild rebound anxiety and insomnia for a few days. Nothing comparable to other depressants on this page.
Addiction & Dependence
Low addiction potential with recreational use. Some heavy daily users develop habitual use but physical dependence is rare. 'Kava dermopathy' (dry scaly skin) develops with chronic heavy use and resolves when use stops. Liver enzyme elevation is possible with very heavy chronic use — take regular breaks.

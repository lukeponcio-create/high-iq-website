KRATOM

Mitragyna speciosa · Biak, Ketum
A Southeast Asian plant with opioid-like alkaloids. Low doses are stimulating and focusing. High doses are sedating and euphoric. Real dependence potential with daily use.
Moderate Risk Onset: 15–30 min (powder) · 30–60 min (capsules) Duration: 3–5 hours
How It Works
Kratom's primary alkaloids — mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine — act as partial agonists at mu-opioid receptors, the same receptors targeted by heroin, oxycodone, and morphine. At low doses (1–3g) the stimulating effects dominate through adrenergic activity. At higher doses (4–6g) the opioid effects take over, producing pain relief, sedation, and euphoria. This dose-dependent flip is unique among drugs.
Dosing
Stimulating 1–3g Energy, focus, mood lift · best for productivity
Sweet spot 3–5g Euphoria, relaxation, sociability
Strong 5–7g Heavy sedation, significant pain relief
Too much 7g+ 'Wobbles' — nausea, dizziness, dysphoria
Redose Wait 3–4hrs Effects last longer than people expect
Dangerous Interactions
☠️
Other opioids
Additive respiratory depression. Kratom + opioids is a dangerous combo regardless of dosage.
☠️
Alcohol
Both CNS depressants. Multiple kratom deaths involved co-use with alcohol.
⚠️
Benzos
Increased sedation and respiratory depression risk at higher doses.
⚠️
MAOIs
Potential serotonin syndrome risk — avoid.
ℹ️
Stimulants
Masks sedation but doesn't reverse it. Adds cardiovascular stress.
Harm Reduction Tips
📅
Max 3x per week
The single most important rule. Daily use creates tolerance and withdrawal faster than most people expect.
🌿
Rotate strains
White (energizing) → Green (balanced) → Red (sedating). Rotating slows tolerance buildup significantly.
🥤
Citrus works
Mix in orange juice — acidity improves absorption and masks the bitter taste.
🧪
Source quality matters
Unregulated market = wildly variable potency and contamination risk. Use vendors who provide lab testing.
⏱️
Capsule delay
Capsules take 30–60 min vs 15 min for powder. Wait before deciding 'it's not working.'
Withdrawal
Kratom withdrawal is real and uncomfortable but not life-threatening. Symptoms: flu-like aches, restless legs, anxiety, insomnia, irritability. Peaks at 2–3 days, largely resolves by day 7. Taper doses over 2–4 weeks to minimize severity.
Addiction & Dependence
Physical dependence develops with daily use — typically within 3–4 weeks. Many people use kratom to manage opioid withdrawal or chronic pain, which carries its own risk of cross-dependence. Loperamide and comfort meds help manage withdrawal symptoms.

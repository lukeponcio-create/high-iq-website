#1 tip: One water per drink. B1 + NAC before bed cuts hangover in half.
How it works
Boosts GABA (inhibitory) and suppresses glutamate (excitatory). Brain physically adapts to this — which is why stopping suddenly after heavy daily use causes seizures.
Hangover help
DHM (dihydromyricetin) before bed, electrolytes on waking, B vitamins. Greasy food is a myth — eat before drinking, not after.
Withdrawal can be fatal. Heavy daily drinkers who stop cold turkey risk seizures and delirium. Taper slowly or get medical help — do not white-knuckle it.

How it works
Binds mu-opioid receptors — same as heroin and oxycodone, just weaker. Low dose is energizing and focusing. High dose is sedating and euphoric. This dual nature is what makes it popular and dangerous.
Strains 101
White vein = stimulating. Green vein = balanced. Red vein = sedating/pain relief. Rotate them to slow tolerance buildup.
Not life-threatening but genuinely miserable — restless legs, anxiety, insomnia, sweating. Taper slowly rather than stopping cold. Worst of it usually passes in 5–7 days.

#1 tip: Onset is slow. Set a 2-hour timer before even thinking about redosing. This is how most ODs happen.
Why it's uniquely dangerous
The gap between a good time and unconscious is less than 0.5ml. GBL is even more potent and faster-acting. Always weigh doses — never eyeball. Label every container clearly. People have died drinking it by accident.
If someone overdoses
Recovery position (on their side). Monitor breathing. Call 911 if breathing slows or stops. GHB overdose looks like deep sleep — do not leave them alone.
GHB withdrawal can be worse than alcohol. Daily heavy users risk psychosis, seizures, and death on withdrawal. This is not an exaggeration. Medical detox required if physically dependent.

#1 tip: Any street pressed pill — fentanyl test strip before touching it. Fake Xanax has killed a lot of people.
Tolerance timeline
Physical dependence can form in as little as 2 weeks of daily use. Recreational use: max once every 2 weeks, no exceptions. The euphoria fades fast but dependence doesn't.
Blackout risk
Benzos cause anterograde amnesia — you'll function and seem fine while having zero memory formation. People redose not remembering they already did. Never use alone.
Benzo withdrawal can cause fatal seizures. If you've been using daily for weeks, do NOT stop cold turkey. Taper with long-acting diazepam (Valium) slowly over months. Protracted withdrawal can last a year+.

#1 tip: Once per week maximum. This rule is not optional — the dependency trap is one of the fastest of any legal substance.
The redose trap
2–4 hour onset means people think it's not working and redose. Then everything hits at once. Set a timer. Do not touch it again until the timer goes off regardless of what you feel.
The day after
Flat mood, anxiety, low energy for 1–2 days after. This rebound is the brain recalibrating. Do not redose to fix it — that's the start of the dependency spiral.
Withdrawal is brutal and prolonged. Severe anxiety, insomnia, psychosis, and suicidal ideation reported. The once-per-week rule isn't about being cautious — it's how you actually avoid this outcome.

#1 tip: Empty stomach only — food kills absorption. If you feel nothing the first time, keep trying on separate days (reverse tolerance is real).
How it works
Kavalactones modulate GABA-A receptors and block voltage-gated ion channels. Produces relaxation and mild euphoria without significant respiratory depression — much safer than other depressants. Used ceremonially in Pacific Island cultures for centuries.
Buying guide
Noble kava strains only. Look for "KL" (kavalactone) content on the label — that's what actually matters. Avoid "tudei" kava which causes harsh side effects.
Genuinely the safest option on this page. No respiratory depression, no meaningful withdrawal, low addiction potential. Main concern with heavy chronic use is liver enzymes — take breaks and don't combine with alcohol.

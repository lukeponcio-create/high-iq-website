Microdosing Protocol Guide 💊

Sub-perceptual dosing for enhanced cognition, creativity, and wellbeing. Science-backed protocols and tracking methods.

What is microdosing? Taking sub-perceptual doses of psychedelics (doses so low you don't "trip") on a regular schedule for cognitive enhancement, mood improvement, and creativity. You function normally - work, drive, socialize - while experiencing subtle benefits. Popular in Silicon Valley, creative industries, and therapeutic contexts.
✨

What to Expect from Microdosing

Microdosing is NOT:

  • A trip or psychedelic experience
  • Visually noticeable
  • Impairing or intoxicating
  • A miracle cure for everything
  • Perceptible to others

Microdosing IS:

  • Subtle mood and cognition enhancement
  • Typically imperceptible while it's happening
  • Effects noticed over days/weeks, not hours
  • Compatible with normal daily life
  • A tool, not a magic pill
🧠

⚠️ Important Reality Check
Microdosing research is still early. While many report benefits, it's not a substitute for therapy, medication, or addressing root causes of problems. Placebo effects are real and powerful. Some people feel nothing. Others experience negative effects. Track carefully and be honest with yourself about what's working.
⚖️

Microdose Amounts

General guideline: 1/10th to 1/20th of a recreational dose.

Psilocybin Mushrooms
Typical range: 0.05g - 0.3g dried
Start with: 0.1g (100mg)
Adjust: Increase by 0.05g if you feel nothing. Decrease if you feel too much (tingling, mild visuals, distraction).
LSD
Typical range: 5-20 micrograms (μg)
Start with: 10μg
Note: Requires volumetric dosing (dissolving in distilled water for accurate measurement)
💡 Finding Your Sweet Spot
The goal is to feel nothing in the moment. Effects should be sub-perceptual - only noticeable when you reflect on your day. If you feel high, distracted, or have visuals, you took too much. That's a mini-dose, not a microdose. Lower it next time.

Fadiman Protocol (Most Popular)

The Fadiman Protocol
Developed by Dr. James Fadiman, psychedelic researcher. The most widely used and researched protocol. Simple, effective, sustainable.

Schedule: Dose every 3rd day

Dose → Rest → Rest → Repeat

How it works:

  • Day 1 (Dose day): Take your microdose in the morning. Go about your day normally. Track your experience.
  • Day 2 (Transition day): No dose. You may still feel residual effects. Some report this is the best day.
  • Day 3 (Normal day): No dose. Back to baseline. Compare how you feel to dose days.
  • Day 4: Dose again. Repeat the cycle.

Duration: Try for 4-8 weeks, then take a 2-4 week break to reset tolerance and assess long-term effects.

Stamets Stack (Advanced)

The Stamets Stack
Created by mycologist Paul Stamets. Combines psilocybin with Lion's Mane mushroom and niacin for enhanced neurogenesis (brain growth). More complex but potentially more powerful for cognitive benefits.

The Stack:

  • Psilocybin mushrooms: 0.1-0.3g
  • Lion's Mane: 500-1000mg (promotes neurogenesis)
  • Niacin (Vitamin B3): 100-200mg (causes temporary flushing, increases circulation)

Schedule: 4 days on, 3 days off

⚠️ About Niacin Flushing
Niacin causes temporary skin flushing (redness, tingling, warmth) for 20-30 minutes. This is normal and harmless, but uncomfortable for some. Start with lower dose (50-100mg) to test your tolerance. Do NOT use "no-flush" niacin - the flush is part of the protocol (increases blood flow to brain).

Tracking Your Progress

Tracking is ESSENTIAL. Without it, you won't know if microdosing is actually helping or if it's placebo. Be scientific about this.

Daily Journal Template

Track these daily (dose days and rest days):

  • Date & Dose: Did you dose today? How much?
  • Mood (1-10): How do you feel overall?
  • Energy (1-10): Physical and mental energy levels
  • Focus (1-10): Ability to concentrate
  • Creativity (1-10): Novel ideas, problem-solving
  • Social (1-10): Quality of interactions, empathy
  • Notable effects: Anything different about today?
  • Side effects: Any negatives? (stomach, anxiety, etc.)
  • Sleep quality: How did you sleep?
💡 Tracking Methods
Apps: Mindbloom, Notion templates, habit tracking apps
Spreadsheet: Simple Google Sheet with daily entries
Journal: Physical notebook works great
Voice memos: Quick daily check-ins

The key is consistency. Track every day for at least 4 weeks before deciding if it's working.

After 4-8 weeks, ask yourself:

  • Are dose days noticeably better than rest days?
  • Has my baseline improved overall?
  • Are the benefits worth the effort and cost?
  • Am I experiencing any negative side effects?
  • Do I feel dependent on it? (red flag)

Be honest. If you're not seeing real benefits, it's okay to stop. Microdosing isn't for everyone.

Best Practices & Tips

  • Dose in the morning: Avoid sleep disruption. Take with or after breakfast to reduce nausea.
  • Use a scale: Precision matters. Get a 0.001g scale for accurate dosing.
  • Grind it up: Mushroom potency varies cap to cap. Grind and mix for consistency.
  • Capsule it: Pre-fill capsules for convenience and consistency.
  • Start low: You can always increase. Hard to decrease mid-day.
  • Track honestly: Don't fool yourself. Be objective about effects.
  • Take breaks: 4-8 weeks on, 2-4 weeks off minimum. Tolerance builds.
  • Combine with healthy habits: Meditation, exercise, good sleep amplify benefits.
  • Don't dose every day: You'll build tolerance fast and stop noticing effects.
  • Tell someone: Partner, close friend. Accountability and outside perspective help.
🛑

When to Stop Microdosing

Stop immediately if:

  • Increased anxiety or paranoia
  • Feeling emotionally numb or disconnected
  • Sleep problems that don't resolve
  • Heart palpitations or chest discomfort
  • Feeling dependent on it
  • Worsening of pre-existing mental health conditions
  • Persistent negative side effects

Take a break if:

  • You've been microdosing for 8+ weeks straight
  • Effects have diminished (tolerance)
  • You're not seeing benefits anymore
  • Life circumstances change (pregnancy, new meds, etc.)
  • You want to assess if the benefits were real
⚠️ Not for Everyone
Microdosing doesn't work for everyone. 20-30% of people report no benefits. Others experience negative effects. If you're in that group, that's okay. Psychedelics aren't the only tool for growth and healing. Don't force it.

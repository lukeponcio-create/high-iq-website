First Timer's Guide 🌱

Everything you need to know for your first psychedelic experience. Start here if you've never tripped before.

Welcome! You're about to embark on one of the most profound experiences available to human consciousness. This guide will help you have a safe, positive first trip. Read everything carefully, follow the recommendations, and trust the process. You've got this.
🤔

Are You Ready?

Answer these honestly:

  • Are you at least 21 years old? (Brain development matters)
  • Are you in a stable mental state right now?
  • No personal or family history of schizophrenia or psychosis?
  • Not currently taking SSRIs, lithium, or MAOIs?
  • Can you dedicate a full day with no responsibilities?
  • Do you have a trusted sober sitter available?
  • Are you doing this for curiosity/growth, not to escape problems?

If you answered YES to all: Great! You're ready. Keep reading.

If you answered NO to any: Wait. Address those concerns first. Psychedelics will still be here when the time is right.

⚠️ Don't Trip If:
- You're under 21 (seriously, your brain is still developing)
- You're in active crisis or severe depression
- You have schizophrenia or strong family history of it
- You're on certain medications (SSRIs reduce effects, lithium is dangerous)
- You're pregnant or breastfeeding
- You don't have a trusted sitter for your first time
- You're trying to escape problems rather than face them
⚖️

Beginner Dosages

The #1 rule for first-timers: START LOW. You can always take more next time. You can't undo taking too much.

Dose Level Dried Mushrooms What to Expect
Microdose 0.1-0.3g Subtle mood lift, no visuals. Not a "trip." Good for first exposure to the substance.
Threshold 0.5-1g Mild effects, slight visuals, enhanced colors. Clear-headed. Very manageable.
Light/Low Recommended for First-Timers 1-1.5g Noticeable visuals, euphoria, some introspection. Still very controllable. Perfect introduction.
Moderate 2-2.5g Full psychedelic experience. Strong visuals, deep thoughts. Only if you feel ready after researching.
Strong 3.5g+ Intense, potentially overwhelming for first-timers. Save this for later trips.
💡 Our Recommendation
For your first time: 1-1.5 grams of dried psilocybin mushrooms. This is the sweet spot - enough to experience the magic without being overwhelming. You'll get visuals, euphoria, and insights, but you'll still feel in control. You can always explore higher doses later once you know how you respond.
🍄

Best Strains for Beginners

Not all mushroom strains are equal. These are the most forgiving for first-timers:

Golden Teacher (Best for First-Timers)
Named for its gentle, teaching nature. Provides a balanced, manageable experience with mild visuals and deep introspection. Most recommended strain for beginners. Moderate potency (0.6-0.8% psilocybin).
B+ (Be Positive)
Known for consistently positive experiences. Uplifting, social, less intense than average. Great if you want something gentle and fun. Moderate potency (0.6-0.7%).
Mazatapec
Ancient shamanic strain. Very gentle, spiritual, and forgiving. Lowest intensity of common strains. Perfect for those who want a mild, healing experience. Lower potency (0.4-0.6%).
⚠️ Avoid These for Your First Time
Penis Envy, Albino Penis Envy, McKennaii: These are 50-100% more potent than average strains. Wait until you have experience. Starting with these is like learning to drive in a Ferrari - technically possible, but not recommended.

What Will It Actually Feel Like?

At a beginner dose (1-1.5g), here's what to expect:

Physical sensations:

  • Body feeling lighter or slightly heavy
  • Mild stomach butterflies (first 30 mins, then fades)
  • Enhanced sense of touch
  • Maybe some yawning
  • Everything feels more tactile and interesting

Visual changes:

  • Colors become more vibrant and saturated
  • Patterns in textures (wood grain, carpet) seem to move or breathe
  • Mild geometric patterns with eyes closed
  • Enhanced appreciation for nature and art
  • NOT like cartoon hallucinations - you won't see things that aren't there

Mental/emotional:

  • Sense of wonder and childlike curiosity
  • Thoughts flowing differently - creative connections
  • Laughing at things that aren't usually funny
  • Feeling connected to people/nature
  • Maybe some introspective realizations
  • Sense that everything is profoundly meaningful

Duration: 4-6 hours from dose to mostly back to normal.

💡 Managing Expectations
At a beginner dose, you'll be surprised how "normal" you still feel. You won't lose touch with reality. You'll know you took mushrooms. You can have conversations. It's intense but manageable - that's the point. Higher doses are where things get wild.

Common First-Timer Mistakes

Learn from others' mistakes:

Taking Too Much
Ego says "I can handle it." But intensity isn't the goal. Start low. You can always go higher next time. Too much your first time can be traumatic and turn you off psychedelics forever.
Not Having a Sitter
"I'll be fine alone." Maybe. But what if you're not? Having a trusted person there eliminates 90% of potential bad experiences. First-timers NEED a sitter. Non-negotiable.
Bad Setting
Tripping at a party, in public, or somewhere you might be interrupted. Your first time should be in a safe, comfortable, private space. Save adventures for later.
Fighting the Experience
Trying to stay in control, resisting the effects, panicking when things get weird. Surrender is key. If you fight it, you'll have a hard time. Let go and flow with it.
No Preparation
Just winging it without research, planning, or supplies. Preparation reduces anxiety. Read this guide, get your space ready, have everything you need. Don't improvise your first time.
Mixing Substances
Adding alcohol, weed, or other drugs to the mix. Keep it pure your first time. Learn how mushrooms affect YOU before adding variables. Alcohol dulls the experience anyway.
Ignoring Set and Setting
Going in with a bad mindset or in a chaotic environment. Your mental state and surroundings MASSIVELY impact the experience. Wait for the right time and place.

First-Timer FAQ

Will I lose control and do something crazy?
At a beginner dose? No. You'll be conscious and aware. You might feel silly or emotional, but you won't lose your mind. That's Hollywood nonsense. You can still think, talk, and make decisions.
Can I die from mushrooms?
Psilocybin itself has an extremely high safety profile - there's never been a documented fatal overdose from psilocybin alone. The lethal dose is hundreds of times higher than an active dose. The real risks are behavioral (doing something unsafe while tripping) or mixing with other substances. This is why you need a sitter.
What if I have a bad trip?
With proper preparation, good set/setting, a beginner dose, and a sitter, bad trips are rare. If you do have difficult moments (more likely), they pass. Breathe, surrender, change the music, talk to your sitter. It's temporary. Read our Safety guide for de-escalation techniques.
Will it change me permanently?
Psychedelics can shift perspectives, but you're still you. Many report positive lasting changes - more appreciation for life, less anxiety, better relationships. But you won't become a different person. Integration (journaling, processing) helps make changes stick.
How do I know my mushrooms are real/safe?
Ideally, know your source. Psilocybin mushrooms have distinctive features (blue bruising, purple-brown spore prints). If uncertain, start with a very small amount to test. There are also test kits available. Never buy random mushrooms from strangers.
Can I work/drive the next day?
Clear your schedule for the day after too. You'll likely feel fine but might be emotionally sensitive or tired. Don't plan anything important. Give yourself integration time.
Do I need to fast before?
Not required, but eating light or fasting 4-6 hours reduces nausea. Empty stomach = faster onset, possibly more intensity. Light stomach = gentler come-up. Your choice.

First Trip Checklist

Before you take mushrooms, make sure you have:

  • ✓ Read this entire guide
  • ✓ Read the Preparation guide
  • ✓ Chosen a beginner-friendly strain (Golden Teacher, B+, or Mazatapec)
  • ✓ Measured out 1-1.5 grams (use a scale)
  • ✓ Confirmed a trusted sitter is available
  • ✓ Cleared your schedule (day of + day after)
  • ✓ Prepared your space (comfortable, safe, private)
  • ✓ Made a 6-8 hour music playlist
  • ✓ Gathered supplies (water, blankets, snacks, journal)
  • ✓ Checked you're in a good mental state
  • ✓ Set an intention (optional but recommended)
  • ✓ Told yourself you'll surrender and trust the process

All checked? You're ready. Take a deep breath. You've got this. 🍄

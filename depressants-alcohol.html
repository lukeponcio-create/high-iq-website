ALCOHOL

Ethanol · Beer, Wine, Spirits
The world's most widely used recreational drug. GABA enhancer, glutamate blocker, socially normalized, and genuinely dangerous with other depressants.
High Risk Onset: 15–30 min Duration: 1–2 hrs per drink
How It Works
Alcohol binds GABA-A receptors and enhances their inhibitory effect while simultaneously blocking NMDA glutamate receptors. The result is CNS-wide depression — slower reaction time, reduced inhibition, impaired coordination, and at high doses respiratory depression. Long-term daily use physically restructures these receptor systems, which is why stopping abruptly can cause fatal seizures.
Dosing
Threshold 1 drink 0.02–0.04 BAC · mild warmth
Buzz 2–3 drinks 0.04–0.07 BAC · relaxed, social
Tipsy 3–5 drinks 0.07–0.10 BAC · impaired coordination
Drunk 5–8 drinks 0.10–0.15 BAC · slurred speech
Blackout zone 8+ drinks 0.15+ BAC · memory formation stops
Overdose risk 0.25+ BAC Vomiting, unconsciousness, death
Dangerous Interactions
☠️
Benzos / Xanax
Both enhance GABA. Respiratory depression multiplies — not adds. This combo sends hundreds to the ER weekly.
☠️
Opioids
Synergistic respiratory depression. Most overdose deaths in the US involve this combination.
☠️
GHB / GBL
Extremely dangerous. Even 1–2 drinks with GHB dramatically increases OD risk. Responsible for most GHB deaths.
⚠️
Cocaine / Stimulants
Stimulants mask intoxication level — you drink far more than you realize and feel it all at once when they wear off.
⚠️
Cannabis
'Greening out' — intense nausea, anxiety, spinning. Very common combo gone wrong.
ℹ️
Kratom
Both are CNS depressants. Multiple kratom deaths involved alcohol co-use.
Harm Reduction Tips
💧
One water per drink
Cuts hangover severity significantly. Set it as a rule before you start.
🍔
Eat a heavy meal first
Slows absorption dramatically. Not after — before.
🧬
Hangover stack
B1 (thiamine) + NAC before bed, DHM (dihydromyricetin) before/during, electrolytes on waking. Actually works.
🚰
Recovery position
Unconscious person — roll them on their side. Prevents aspirating vomit. Could save a life.
📏
Count your drinks
Sounds obvious. Nobody actually does it. Use your phone notes.
Withdrawal
FATAL RISK: Alcohol withdrawal can cause life-threatening seizures and delirium tremens (DTs). Heavy daily drinkers should NEVER stop cold turkey. Taper slowly or seek medical supervision. This is not optional.
Addiction & Dependence
Tolerance builds fast. Physical dependence can develop within weeks of daily heavy use. Alcohol use disorder is one of the most common and most treatable substance use disorders — options include medication (naltrexone, acamprosate), therapy, and support groups.

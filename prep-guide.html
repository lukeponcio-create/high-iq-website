Trip Preparation Guide 🎒

Proper preparation is the foundation of a safe, meaningful psychedelic experience. This guide covers everything you need before your journey.

Why Preparation Matters: Research from Johns Hopkins and MAPS consistently shows that set (mindset), setting (environment), and preparation dramatically impact psychedelic experiences. Taking time to prepare can be the difference between a transformative journey and an overwhelming one.
🧠

Set: Your Mindset

"Set" refers to your mental and emotional state going into the experience. Your mindset shapes the journey.

✓
Choose the Right Day
Pick a day when you're feeling emotionally stable, well-rested, and have nothing stressful scheduled for 24-48 hours. Don't trip to escape problems.
✓
Set Your Intention
What do you hope to explore or learn? Not required, but having a gentle intention (not rigid expectation) can guide your experience. Examples: "I want to understand myself better" or "I want to feel more connected to nature."
✓
Mental Health Check
If you're dealing with active psychosis, severe depression, or unprocessed trauma, work with a therapist first. Psychedelics amplify your current state.
✓
Let Go of Control
The biggest mistake is trying to control the experience. Go in with openness and surrender. "Let go or be dragged" - ancient wisdom applies here.
⚠️ Don't Trip If:
- You're under 21 (brain still developing)
- You have personal or family history of schizophrenia/psychosis
- You're taking certain medications (SSRIs, lithium, tramadol - check interactions)
- You're pregnant or breastfeeding
- You're in a bad mental state or facing a crisis
🏡

Setting: Your Environment

"Setting" is your physical environment. Create a space that feels safe, comfortable, and supports your journey.

✓
Choose Your Location Wisely
Ideally: A comfortable, familiar space where you won't be interrupted. Nature is beautiful but save it for when you're experienced. Avoid public places your first time.
✓
Clear Your Schedule
Block out 8-10 hours for the experience, plus the next day for rest and integration. Turn off notifications. Tell roommates/family you need privacy.
✓
Have a Trip Sitter (First Time)
Someone sober, experienced, and trustworthy who can stay with you. They don't need to talk much - just be present and help if needed. CRITICAL for first-timers or high doses.
✓
Control the Vibe
Dim lighting (no harsh fluorescents), comfortable temperature, soft blankets, pillows. Make it cozy. You want to feel like you're in a safe nest.
💡 Pro Tip: The Comfort Test
Before your trip, spend 30 minutes in your chosen space just sitting quietly. Does it feel peaceful? Are there distractions? Adjust until it feels right. Trust your gut.
🎒

Essential Supplies

Having these items ready eliminates stress during your journey. Prepare everything before you dose.

💧
Water
Stay hydrated. Room temp is easier on the stomach.
🍊
Light Snacks
Fruit, crackers. You might not be hungry but have options.
🎵
Music Playlist
6-8 hours of calming, instrumental music. See playlist section below.
🛋️
Blankets
For comfort and warmth. Weighted blankets are amazing.
🪣
Bucket
Nausea can happen. Better safe than sorry.
📔
Journal & Pen
For post-trip integration. Not during - wait until after.
📱
Phone (Off)
Turn it off or airplane mode. But keep nearby for emergencies.
😎
Eye Mask
For deeper introspection. Blocks visual distractions.
🎧

Music & Playlists

Music profoundly shapes psychedelic experiences. Johns Hopkins uses specific playlists in their research for a reason.

What Works Best:

  • Instrumental music (no lyrics to get stuck on)
  • Classical, ambient, nature sounds
  • Music that builds and releases (matches the trip arc)
  • Familiar music you love, or discovery mode

Avoid: Heavy metal, aggressive rap, anything jarring or anxiety-inducing. Save experimental music for later trips.

🎵 Recommended Playlists
- Johns Hopkins Psilocybin Playlist (the official research playlist)
- Wavepaths (AI-generated psychedelic music)
- Jon Hopkins - Music for Psychedelic Therapy
- Nature sounds: Rain, ocean waves, forest ambience
- Classical: Beethoven, Debussy, Satie
📋

Day-Of Checklist

Final preparations before you begin your journey:

✓
Eat Light (or Fast)
Empty or light stomach reduces nausea. Some people fast 4-6 hours before. Others eat lightly. No heavy meals.
✓
Confirm Your Sitter
Double-check they're available and committed. Go over their role: stay calm, don't interfere unless asked, be present.
✓
Test Your Dose
Use a scale. Know exactly how much you're taking. Start low - you can always try more another time.
✓
Set Up Your Space
Everything in place: water, snacks, blankets, music queued up, phone off. Nothing to do but experience.
✓
One Last Reminder
Whatever happens, it's temporary. You will return. Surrender and trust the process. You've prepared well.
🚫

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Taking too much your first time - Start low, be patient
  • Mixing substances - No alcohol, weed (for first-timers), or other drugs
  • Tripping in public - Wait until you're experienced
  • Not having a sitter - First-timers need support
  • Fighting the experience - Surrender is key
  • Looking at screens - Social media can be jarring and anxious
  • Having expectations - Let it unfold naturally
  • Tripping when stressed - It amplifies everything

BENZOS

Benzodiazepines · Xanax, Valium, Klonopin, Ativan
Prescription CNS depressants with real recreational appeal — and some of the most dangerous dependence and withdrawal profiles of any drug class. Street pills often contain fentanyl.
Critical Risk Onset: 15–30 min (varies by compound) Duration: 4–8 hrs (Xanax) · 12–24 hrs (Valium)
How It Works
Benzos are positive allosteric modulators of GABA-A receptors — they bind to a site distinct from alcohol and enhance the effect of GABA by increasing chloride ion flow. This produces anxiolysis, muscle relaxation, sedation, and at higher doses amnesia and euphoria. Tolerance develops rapidly, often within days of daily use, forcing dose escalation. This tolerance is physical — the brain downregulates GABA receptors — which creates withdrawal risk.
Dosing
Xanax (alprazolam) 0.25–1mg Short-acting · high addiction potential
Valium (diazepam) 5–15mg Long-acting · smoother · easier to taper
Klonopin (clonazepam) 0.5–2mg Medium duration
Ativan (lorazepam) 0.5–2mg Medium duration · common ER use
Street 'bars' UNKNOWN ⚠️ Test with fentanyl strips — mandatory
Dangerous Interactions
☠️
Alcohol
The most common deadly drug combination. Both enhance GABA. Respiratory depression, blackouts, death. No safe amount together.
☠️
Opioids
FDA black box warning. Leading cause of overdose deaths in the US. The combination suppresses breathing synergistically.
☠️
GHB / Phenibut
All are GABAergic. The combined CNS depression is unpredictable and dangerous.
☠️
Kratom
Additive sedation. Increased respiratory risk, especially at higher doses of either.
⚠️
Stimulants
Masks sedation level — people take more benzo thinking they're less affected than they are.
Harm Reduction Tips
🧪
Fentanyl test every time
Street pressed Xanax bars are frequently contaminated with fentanyl. This is not rare — it's extremely common. Test strip before every use, no exceptions.
📅
Once per 2 weeks max
Physical dependence can form in 2–4 weeks of daily use. This frequency limit is how you actually stay recreational rather than dependent.
🚫
Zero alcohol
The benzo + alcohol combination is dangerous at doses that seem fine separately. Not 'drink less' — zero.
👥
Never alone
Amnesia is common — you won't remember redosing. Someone needs to be present.
⬇️
If tapering off
Switch to long-acting diazepam (Valium) and reduce 5–10% per week. Never stop cold turkey if you've been using daily.
Withdrawal
POTENTIALLY FATAL: Benzo withdrawal can cause life-threatening seizures — same mechanism as alcohol withdrawal. If you've used daily for weeks or more, never stop cold turkey. A slow taper using long-acting diazepam under medical supervision is standard. Protracted withdrawal syndrome (PAWS) with anxiety and insomnia can persist for months to years.
Addiction & Dependence
Benzos are one of the most dependency-forming drug classes. The therapeutic window closes quickly as tolerance develops — the same dose that relieved anxiety last month may barely touch it now, while withdrawal symptoms fill the gaps between doses. Many people don't realize they're dependent until they try to stop.

GHB / GBL

Gamma-Hydroxybutyrate · Liquid Ecstasy · G
An endogenous neurotransmitter with a recreational dose window so narrow it has killed people at amounts barely above the recreational range. The no-alcohol rule is absolute.
Critical Risk Onset: 20–45 min (slow — the main redose trap) Duration: 1.5–3 hours
How It Works
GHB is actually produced naturally in the human brain in small amounts. Recreationally, it acts primarily on GABA-B receptors (similar to baclofen) and dedicated GHB receptors. At 1–2g it produces euphoria, reduced inhibition, and increased sociability. At 3–4g it causes heavy sedation and memory loss. At 4g+ it causes unconsciousness. The danger is that this entire spectrum spans less than 2–3g — a difference measurable in drops of liquid GBL.
Dosing
Threshold 0.5–1g Mild relaxation, warmth
Recreational 1–2.5g Euphoria, sociability, disinhibition
Strong 2.5–4g Heavy sedation, memory impairment
Overdose zone 4g+ Unconsciousness, vomiting, respiratory risk
GBL equiv. ~0.75x GHB dose More potent, faster onset — dose lower
Dangerous Interactions
☠️
Alcohol — NEVER
Even 1–2 drinks exponentially increases OD risk. Responsible for the majority of GHB-related deaths. This is not a 'be careful' situation — it's a hard no.
☠️
Benzos
Triply sedating. Respiratory arrest risk.
☠️
Opioids
Severe CNS depression. Fatal combinations have occurred at doses that would be safe separately.
☠️
Ketamine
Both dissociative/sedating. Unpredictable and dangerous interaction.
⚠️
MDMA
Popular combination but MDMA masks GHB's warning signs — you may not realize you're approaching overdose.
Harm Reduction Tips
⚖️
Weigh every dose
Use a precise scale or oral syringe. Never eyeball GHB/GBL — concentration varies and the margin for error is measured in fractions of a ml.
⏱️
2-hour redose timer
Set it before you take the first dose. GHB's slow onset causes people to redose thinking it's not working — then both doses hit simultaneously.
🏷️
Label everything
People have accidentally drunk GHB thinking it was water. Every container, always.
👥
Never use alone
If someone loses consciousness, put them in the recovery position and monitor breathing. Call 911 if breathing slows.
🚫
Zero alcohol rule
The interaction is exponential not additive. Not 'less alcohol' — zero alcohol on GHB nights.
Withdrawal
SEVERE: GHB withdrawal in daily heavy users can be worse than alcohol withdrawal — psychosis, severe anxiety, tremors, and potentially fatal seizures. Daily use for weeks creates physical dependence. Medical detox is essential. This is one of the most dangerous drug withdrawals that exists.
Addiction & Dependence
GHB's short duration (1.5–3 hrs) means heavy users need to dose every few hours including through the night, which rapidly leads to physical dependence. The combination of high addiction potential, narrow dose window, and severe withdrawal makes GHB one of the highest-risk recreational drugs available.
